Meet Paul Anderson

Builder Capital Group provides reliable, fast, and competitive debt and equity solutions for real estate developers, homebuilders, and commercial investors nationwide.

We specialize in matching each project with the right private, institutional, or alternative capital provider based on its specific financing requirements.

Our financing solutions include ground-up construction, residential subdivisions, commercial infill, bridge and mezzanine financing, land and pre-development capital, and permanent or refinance debt.

We provide end-to-end deal advisory, including deal packaging, capital stack structuring, and lender negotiations from pre-development through stabilization or exit.
